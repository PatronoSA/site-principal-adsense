1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to website owners.

Cookies cannot run programs or deliver viruses to your computer. They are uniquely assigned to you and can only be read by a web server in the domain that issued the cookie.

4. How to Control Cookies

You have several options to control or limit how cookies are used:

Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to view, manage, delete, and block cookies. Note that if you delete or block cookies, some features of our website may not work correctly. Find instructions for popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Our cookie banner: When you first visit our site, a cookie consent banner is displayed. You may click Accept to allow all cookies or Decline to opt out of non-essential cookies. You can reset this preference at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for lifemasterypath.com.

Opt-out tools:

  • Google Ads Settings — opt out of personalized Google ads
  • Google Analytics Opt-out — prevent Google Analytics tracking
  • Digital Advertising Alliance — opt out of interest-based advertising
  • Your Online Choices (EU users)

5. Do Not Track

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want to be tracked. Because there is no consistent industry standard for DNT, our website does not currently respond to DNT signals. However, you can use the opt-out tools listed above to limit tracking.
